- **What didn't:** Partially contradicted. The consolidation premise holds — people stay in one surface for long sessions and do progress candidates through stages, no bouncing. But the kill-switch criterion (≥60% complete review → screen → schedule → offer → hire in this interface) is not met: only 1 of 4 tracked candidates reached hire, and zero passed through a real scheduling action. Two candidates sit at offer having bypassed the invite step entirely.

That points to a different problem than tab-switching: scheduling is where the workflow leaks out of the product, and the candidate-review loop fatigues after ~3 profiles. Worth re-framing M2 around "the interview handoff is the friction point," not "tab switching," and instrumenting explicit stage-transition and AI-confirm events so this isn't inferred from row counts.
