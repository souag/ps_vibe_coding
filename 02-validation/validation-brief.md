# Validation Brief

**Scenario:** Scenario 1 - Custom Application Tracking System (ATS) for recruiters

## 1 · Hypothesis
I'm testing whether recruiters can use a single tool and all hiring purpose without having to swivel between multiple tool.

## 2 · Risk type & kill switch
- **Risk type:** Usability
- **Build to test it:** Clickable mockup
- **Kill switch:** Enable a single pane of glass to recruiters for their day to day job

## 3 · Success criteria
- Recruiters are able to see all required information in a single system
- Reduce time to hire
- Agentic experience

## 4 · Problem Framework
1. **Goal**, Reduce time to hire
Improved experience
2. **Problem**, Information spread across multiple systems
3. **Context**, Prototype should improve experience and provide the right insights to recruiters
4. **Constraints**, Current system are SaaS tools and not owned by the company
5. **Success**, Recruiter CSAT improves by 10% in year 1; 15% in yea year 2
Time to Hire reduces by 10%
6. **Explore**, Perform rapid prototyping to determine the user requirements and the feasibility and usability of the new tool
